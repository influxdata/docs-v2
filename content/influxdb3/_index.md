InfluxDB 3 is the current generation of InfluxDB and the recommended platform
for new time series workloads that need its capabilities.
Built on Apache Arrow and object storage, it supports SQL and InfluxQL,
handles unlimited series cardinality, and scales without managing local disks.
If you run InfluxDB 2, see the guide to [evaluate whether InfluxDB 3 fits your workload](/influxdb3/which-influxdb-3/#coming-from-influxdb-1-or-influxdb-2).
InfluxDB 1 remains available and actively supported.
To choose between InfluxDB 1 and InfluxDB 3, see [InfluxDB 1](/platform/#influxdb-1x)
and [Which InfluxDB 3 should I use?](/influxdb3/which-influxdb-3/).
